About this calculator

A general-purpose techno-economic analysis (TEA) tool for two-stage anaerobic digestion (TSAD) biohythane production systems. Enter your own feedstock parameters and receive NPV, CAPEX, OPEX, payback period, and ROI calculations — works for any organic substrate.

Methodology

  • CAPEX: six-tenths rule with Lang factor and CEPCI escalation
  • OPEX: maintenance + insurance + labour + electricity + chemicals
  • Mass balance: yield-basis or rate-basis gas production
  • H₂ density: 0.0899 kg/m³ at STP for revenue conversion
  • NPV: discounted cash flow over project lifetime
  • Sensitivity: ±30% on key variables

Key references

Bhatt & Tao (2020). Bioengineering 7(3), 74 — CAPEX methodology, Lang factor, six-tenths rule
Mahmod et al. (2021). J. Environ. Chem. Eng. 9(4), 105679 — TSAD biohythane TEA benchmark

Tabassum et al. (2024). Environ. Sci. Pollut. Res. — H₂ price range, biohythane TEA review

Default values

Defaults are based on four TSAD source papers from Feng Chia University (Taiwan): Abdul et al. (2025), Lin et al. (2021a,b), Lay et al. (2013). All monetary values in 2023 USD.
